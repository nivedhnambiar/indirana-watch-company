Our heritage

Designed for those who carry quiet confidence.

Indirana was born from a love of classic Indian artistry and the precision of modern engineering. Each watch is shaped to honor age-old craftsmanship while fitting effortlessly into a contemporary lifestyle.

From the first sketch to the final polish, every detail reflects a belief that luxury should be meaningful, enduring, and beautifully honest.
