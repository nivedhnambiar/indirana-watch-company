Journal

Notes from the bench

Style guide

Which strap colour goes with what

Tan suits cotton, linen and weekend wear. Oxblood works with suits and formal Indian wear. Black is the evening choice. Olive sits well with denim and earth tones. Every model comes in all four.

Materials

Why leather darkens with wear

Vegetable-tanned leather takes in oils from your skin and colour from daylight, so it darkens and gains a soft sheen over the first few months. This is normal and part of the strap’s character. Keep it dry and let it air after wet days.

Dials

How to read a Devanagari dial

Devanagari numerals use the same positional system as the digits you know: १ is 1, २ is 2, ३ is 3, ४ is 4, ५ is 5, ६ is 6, ७ is 7, ८ is 8, ९ is 9 and ० is 0. So १२ at the top of the dial is twelve, ३ is three, ६ is six and ९ is nine.
